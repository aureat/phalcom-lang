The tape — and where slot numbers come from

A local access is one addition: a number the compiler baked into the instruction, plus the frame’s base. Three panes, one fact.

Reconstructed fixture — two things are not verified against the tree. (1) The bytecode is reconstructed, not disassembled — opcode names and operand shapes follow bytecode.rs, but the exact emission sequence was written by hand. (2) The claim that let total = c.tick(3) lands its result in the slot that becomes total (rather than pre-pushing an empty slot) is inferred from the Lua/Wren lineage. Confirm both with phalcom disasm on a module-level hoist before this becomes a real fixture. The arithmetic itself is verified: dispatch.rs:721 is stack_offset + slot.
