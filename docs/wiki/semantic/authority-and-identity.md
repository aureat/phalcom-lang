# Semantic authority and identity

> Sources: semantic-analyzer chapters 02, 09–11; SEMA002
> Raw: [semantic specification and program snapshot](../raw/semantic/2026-09-08-semantic-sources.md)
> Updated: 2026-09-08

Semantic authority answers which facts may support a formal judgment and which identities remain stable across requests. It neighbors module identity and source locations but owns neither project resolution nor terminal rendering.

## Evidence

Established, assumed, unknown, and dynamic knowledge are distinct. Advisory runtime-shape observations may enrich presentation or optimization hypotheses, but cannot strengthen formal dispatch, subtype, or callable judgments. Relation outcomes are reconciled by the owning semantic lane.

## Identity

Semantic, source, revision, declaration, and attachment identities are separate dimensions. Source sites preserve origin; snapshot/revision identity guards consumer consistency. Metadata fingerprints support comparison and reuse but are not authority on their own.

SEMA002 is in progress and partial. [Semantic metadata](../type-system/semantic-type-metadata.md) serializes the resulting identities; [editor snapshots](../editor/workspace-snapshots-and-incrementality.md) pin them for consumers.
